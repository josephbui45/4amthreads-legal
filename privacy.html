Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 19, 2026

4amthreads respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data.

1. Data Collection

We collect only the minimum data necessary to authenticate with TikTok and publish videos on your behalf. This includes your TikTok authentication token.

2. Data Usage

Your data is used solely for the purpose of posting video content to your TikTok account. We do not sell, share, or distribute your data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers beyond TikTok's API.

4. Data Deletion

You can revoke access at any time by removing the app from your TikTok account settings. All local data can be deleted by removing the application files.

5. Contact

For privacy concerns, reach out via GitHub: github.com/josephbui45
